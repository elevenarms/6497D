    An Exercise in Assembly

    Assembling the 6497

    On the bench before you lie the parts of an ETA 6497, a hand-wound Lépine calibre of seventeen jewels. Your task is to put the movement together in the order the maker lays down, from the bare main plate to the hour wheel, and then to wind it and set it going.

    • Take up the next part from the List of Components. Choosing a part out of order counts as a fault.
    • Move it over its place, which is shown in shadow on the figure, and click to set it down. Enter sets it for you, and Esc puts it back.
    • Drag the figure to turn it and scroll to zoom. The movement turns over by itself when the work changes sides.
    • When it is complete, Wind the watch, Pull Crown to set the hands, and run it at up to ×3600.
    • To watch the work done for you, use Automatic Assembly or Assemble & Run the Watch.
